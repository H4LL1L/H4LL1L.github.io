A brass bell, a blue crab, a key lost in the market. What will you spot first?

Hidden Corner is a hidden object puzzle set in two illustrated seaside maps. Start at Geranium Pier, then search Lantern Market for a striped cup, a star lantern and a brass key.

Check your target list, move around the map and zoom in when a detail catches your eye. Tap the object when you find it. Finish the pier to unlock the market, or take a break and pick up where you left off.

Explore at your own pace and play offline.
