One good drop can set off a whole chain. Choose where each piece lands, merge matching items into bigger ones, and make room for your next move.

Dropmelo is a merge puzzle about making space as the board fills up. Set up one match to create another. The longer you keep the chain going, the further you can push your score.

Try different themed modes, unlock badges as you play and come back for another run. How high can you score before you run out of room?
