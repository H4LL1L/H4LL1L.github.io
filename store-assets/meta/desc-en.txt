One good drop can set off a whole chain. Choose where each piece lands, merge matching pieces into bigger ones and make room for your next move.

Dropmelo is a relaxing merge puzzle with real physics. Drop, match and merge: two identical pieces combine into the next one up. Keep the chain going, build combos and push your high score higher before the board fills up.

WHY YOU'LL LOVE DROPMELO
• Simple to learn: tap to drop, watch pieces roll, bounce and merge
• 8 themes, one game: Sweet Café, Kitchen, Computer, School, Car Garage, Sea Aquarium, Flower Garden and Cell
• Chain combos: one smart drop can trigger a cascade of merges
• Jokers when you need them: Bomb, Enlarge and Shake help you clear a crowded board
• Decorate your rooms with the stars you earn
• Daily rewards, quests and the limited-time Merge Rush event
• Leaderboards: see how your best score ranks
• Play offline: no internet needed to start a run

If you enjoy fruit merge, ball merge, drop merge or 2048-style puzzles, Dropmelo is your next favorite casual game. How high can you score before you run out of room?

Dropmelo is free to play and contains optional in-app purchases and ads.
