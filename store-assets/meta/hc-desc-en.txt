A brass bell, a blue crab, a key lost in the market. What will you spot first?

Hidden Corner is a hidden object game set in hand-illustrated seaside maps. Start at Geranium Pier, then search Lantern Market for a striped cup, a star lantern and a brass key.

HOW TO PLAY
• Check your target list at the bottom of the screen
• Drag to explore the map and pinch to zoom in on the details
• Tap an object when you find it
• Finish the pier to unlock the market

FEATURES
• Detailed, hand-illustrated seaside scenes
• Relaxing seek and find gameplay at your own pace
• Hints when you get stuck
• Daily gift
• Play offline, no internet needed
• Your progress is saved on your device

Note: the game's interface is currently in Turkish.
